Professional program | ISRO

25-Station GNSS Network

I led a $1M satellite-based monitoring network from partner sites and procurement through field deployment. Coordinating 20 people across science, engineering, vendors, and IT, I helped redesign data transmission so observations survived connectivity failures. The team delivered 25 stations on schedule and 10% under budget, saving $700K annually. Store-and-forward recovery cut real-time gaps from 50% to 2% without permanent data loss.

Professional program | ISRO

ISRO Storm Nowcasting

I led a five-person team building an ML storm-forecasting system and coordinated mission stakeholders to turn its forecasts into launch-review guidance. My work connected model validation, review protocols, and senior briefings. The evaluated system reached 97.5% accuracy at a 5% false-alarm rate, extending warning from 15 minutes to two hours across four quarterly launch director reviews.

GreenPack Energy Retrofit

I built a five-year financial and operating model across 80+ retrofit categories, connecting pricing, delivery capacity, and sponsor value. Leadership could compare scenarios of 200 to 900 homes annually and prioritize partners. The business case projected $3.2M in revenue; prioritizing 30 launch partners helped accelerate the pilot by two months.

Independent build | AI workflow

NORA AI Assistant

I designed an assistant with separate retrieval, response validation, and human-review steps. Teams can trace an answer to its sources and see where automation should stop. In a 50-policy-question evaluation, NORA answered over 80% of in-scope questions with the correct source and refused all out-of-scope questions in that set.

Independent build | Live demo

RisQ Weather Intelligence

I built RisQ as an AI weather-risk prototype for last-mile delivery teams. It maps live risk for 55+ U.S. cities: compare forecast stress, check official alerts, and ask SkyScout about the available data.

Node, Vite, TypeScript, and Leaflet connect Open-Meteo forecasts and NOAA alerts to the interface. Explore the working product or the implementation behind it.

How I solve problems

How I move a program forward.

A consistent approach, adapted to the technical and business constraints of each project.

  1. 01

    Frame the decision

    Clarify what must be decided, by whom, and which uncertainty is preventing action.

  2. 02

    Test the evidence

    Test what the data support and expose gaps, assumptions, contradictions, and failure modes.

  3. 03

    Organize delivery

    Map owners, dependencies, incentives, handoffs, risks, and the workflow required to deliver.

  4. 04

    Make it usable

    Build the model, workflow, or product; test it with the people who will use it.
